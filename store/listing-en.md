## Full description (≈2,300 / 4,000 characters)

```
Need a photo under 50 KB for an online form? A passport size photo to print? A clean signature for a job or exam application? Photo Resizer does all of it in seconds – 100% offline, and your photos never leave your phone.

★ COMPRESS TO EXACT KB
Reduce photo size in KB with one tap. Pick 20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB or type any custom size. The image compressor finds the best JPEG quality automatically and only shrinks the pixel size when it has to, so your photo stays as sharp as possible while staying at or under the limit. You see the before and after file size and dimensions before you save.

★ RESIZE PHOTOS
Resize image by pixels (with aspect-ratio lock), by percentage, or with presets like 1280×720, 1920×1080, 1080×1080 and 600×600. Choose "fit inside" or "crop to fill", and save as JPEG or PNG.

★ PASSPORT SIZE PHOTO MAKER
Make ID photos in common sizes: 35×45 mm, 2×2 inch (51×51 mm), 30×40 mm, 40×50 mm, 33×48 mm or any custom size in mm. Pinch and drag to line up the face with the on-screen guide. Optional white background for photos taken against a plain, light wall. Photos are exported at 300 DPI, with an optional max file size.

★ 4×6 PRINT SHEET
Put several copies of your passport photo on one 4×6 inch (10×15 cm) sheet at 300 DPI with light cut guides – for example 8 copies of 35×45 mm – ready for any photo lab or home printer.

★ SIGNATURE CLEANER
Sign on paper, snap a photo, and turn it into a clean black-on-white or transparent PNG. Crop around the signature, adjust the threshold slider to remove shadows and paper texture, choose black or blue ink, auto-trim, and pick an output width.

★ SAVE & SHARE
Save to your gallery (Pictures/PhotoResizer) or share straight to WhatsApp, email or any app.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline – no account, no cloud, no uploads
• Uses the Android photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose
• No storage or media permissions on Android 10 and newer

Great for online application forms, job and exam application photos, visa photo size requirements, ID cards, profile pictures and email attachments.

Note: photo sizes are common presets. Always check the exact requirements of the organisation you are applying to. This app is not affiliated with any government agency.

The app is free and supported by ads.
```
