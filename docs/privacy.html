Photo Resizer: KB & Passport

Privacy Policy – Photo Resizer: KB & Passport

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Photo Resizer: KB & Passport ("the app", package com.offerpk.photoresizer) is developed by OfferPk. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Your photos never leave your device

All image processing — compressing to a KB size, resizing, passport/ID photo cropping, print sheets and signature cleaning — happens entirely on your device. The app:

  • has no account or sign-in,
  • does not upload your photos or any file to us or to any server,
  • does not run its own analytics, and we have no server that stores data about you.

Photos are chosen with the Android system photo picker, so the app only receives the single photo you pick and never gets access to the rest of your gallery. Temporary working files are kept in the app's private cache. Images you save go to Pictures/PhotoResizer on your device; images you share go only where you choose to send them.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – used only by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (see below).
  • Write external storage – requested only on Android 9 and older (API 28 and below) so the app can save your result into the Pictures folder. On Android 10 and newer no storage permission is used.

The app does not request camera, contacts, location, or media-library read permissions.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • app interactions with ads (impressions, clicks) and app/device information (model, OS version, app version),
  • diagnostics such as crash and performance data of the SDK.

This data is processed by Google under Google's policies, not by us:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Consent. Before any ad is requested, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). You can change your choice at any time from About & privacy → Privacy options inside the app (shown where required). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not collect or store personal data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Files you saved stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its cache. Requests about data collected by Google's ad services can be made through Google's tools linked above.

Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

No affiliation

Photo sizes offered in the app are common presets. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any government agency. Always check the exact requirements of the organisation you are applying to.
